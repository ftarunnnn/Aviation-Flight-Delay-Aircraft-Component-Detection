🔄 Pipeline A: Flight Delay ML Prediction

Processes flight operational metrics, temporal features, weather severity, and traffic congestion data through XGBoost & Random Forest regressors/classifiers to estimate departure delay times and categorize risk levels.

🔍 Pipeline B: Aircraft Component Defect Detection

Evaluates high-resolution aircraft inspection panels (fuselage, turbine blades, wing surfaces) using PyTorch CNN and YOLO object detectors to identify defect types, bounding box coordinates, and repair severity.
